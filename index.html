Message from the Deputy Commissioner

Welcome to the official Online Arms License Verification Portal of the Deputy Commissioner's Office, Chaman. In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, the integration of technology is essential to ensure transparency, security, and efficient public service delivery. This platform has been developed to provide citizens with a reliable and user-friendly system for verifying arms licenses with ease and accuracy.

Our objective is to facilitate a secure environment where individuals and relevant authorities can access verified information regarding arms licenses, helping to strengthen lawful compliance and public safety. Through this initiative, we aim to minimize manual processes, reduce delays, and enhance trust by offering real-time verification services.

This portal reflects our commitment to leveraging digital solutions to improve governance and accessibility of essential services. Your cooperation and responsible use of this platform are vital in supporting our efforts to maintain law and order within the community.

Together, let us utilize technology to promote transparency, accountability, and a safer society.

Deputy Commissioner, Chaman
